# StillWant Privacy Policy

Effective: August 8, 2026

StillWant stores purchase decisions on the user's device. The app does not require an account and the developer does not collect, sell, or share personal data.

Apple processes subscription purchases and provides signed StoreKit transaction status to the app. Apple's handling of that information is governed by Apple's privacy policy.

When a user exports a CSV file, the destination they choose controls the exported copy. StillWant does not upload the file.

Questions about this policy can be submitted through the public issue tracker at <https://github.com/9uiLe/stillwant-ios/issues>.
